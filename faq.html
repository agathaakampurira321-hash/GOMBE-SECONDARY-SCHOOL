Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for parents and prospective students.

Where is Gombe Secondary School located?

The school is in Gombe, Nansana Municipality, Wakiso District, Uganda.

Which classes does the school admit?

Admission enquiries cover lower secondary and advanced secondary programmes. Please contact the school for current availability.

What documents are needed for admission?

Applicants should bring previous school reports, a birth certificate and identification documents for the parent or guardian.

What subjects are offered?

The school offers core sciences, languages, humanities, mathematics and entrepreneurship subjects.

Does the school provide co-curricular activities?

Yes. Students may participate in sports, clubs and school talent development programmes.

How can a parent enquire about fees?

Use the contact form and select Fees as the enquiry type, or call the school office.
